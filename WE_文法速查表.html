② 18 類：一句口訣＋你最後一次錯的那句

類別・口訣最後一次錯 → 對
1s 的方向
先找主詞：單數動詞加 s，複數不加		a happy worker go home → a happy worker goes home
long hours causes → long hours cause
2介系詞片語
不照中文猜，整組背		punished by each mistake → punished for every mistake
love matters more than on time → more than timing
3可數名詞漏 s
一個 → a；泛指 → s		hurts family → hurts families
discount → short-term discounts
4詞性用錯
be／feel／stay 後形容詞；our／leading to 後名詞		keeps people safely → keeps people safe
people lived in the country → people living in the country
5a／an 掉了
單數可數不裸奔；句首最容易掉		happy employee goes home → A happy employee goes home
get promotion → get a promotion
6the 加錯／漏加
說得出「哪一個」才加；不可數不加 a		take a legal responsibility → take legal responsibility
a better choice（二選一）→ the better choice
7助動詞／to 後原形
should／can／to／crucial that 後一律原形，⑱ 不放 should		can built → can build
crucial that students should finish → crucial that students finish
8不可數名詞
money／information／work／air／history：不加 a、不加 s		deal with the works → deal with his work
fewer money／a cleaner air → less money／cleaner air
9固定片語
收尾片語整串背，一字不換		a warm and a stable home → a warm and stable home
has more harm than good → does more harm than good
10動詞當主詞 -ing
先加 -ing，-ing 算單數；被動主詞 being＋p.p.		students studying old plays does → Studying old plays does
been watch all the time leads → being watched all the time leads
11and／or
否定用 or；A, B and C；and 兩邊對稱		life is slow and far less pressure → life there is slow and has far less pressure
12leading to 後接名詞
只放名詞；and it 換成 that		a bad temper and this hurts family → a bad temper that hurts families
13比較級
可數 fewer、不可數 less；有 than 就有 more		less working hours is less money → fewer working hours mean less money
matters than discounts → matters more than
14逗號連兩句
兩個完整句：, and／, but；不是換 that		in harmony that fame should never → in harmony, and fame should never
things that cannot find → things they cannot find
15make／let／help／see＋原形
直接原形；allow／push 要 to		makes employees to feel → makes employees feel
allow their children use → allow their children to use
16時態
一般情況現在式；James 做過的過去式；if 子句現在式		James gets marry → James married／got married
if student surffing on internet → If a student surfs the internet
17every／there
every／everything 單數＋動詞 s；「有」＝there is／should be		everythings depend on → everything depends on
there has no wage limits → there should be no wage limit
18改一個掉一個
改完整句從頭念一次再送出		with his families and get along with their parents → with his family and gets along with his parents

③ 送出前五步（每寫完一句跑一次）

  1. 找主詞決定動詞 s：單數加、複數不加；-ing 主詞、everything 算單數。
  2. 每個名詞過三關：可數嗎？一個（a）還是泛指（s）？說得出哪一個才加 the。
  3. be／feel／stay／keep 後是形容詞嗎？our／leading to 後是名詞嗎？
  4. 介系詞和收尾片語是整組背的那個嗎？（to／in a better mood／on／for）
  5. 逗號兩邊是兩個完整句嗎？是就補 , and／, but。
