一、解題技巧（做題順序）

⭐ 1. 插入語先遮掉

空格前後有插進來的小片語（from it、in fact、for that matter、逗號夾住的一段），先遮掉再判斷。 
must take this line of symbols and [from it] ____ the original wholeness
→ 遮掉 from it：must take … and ____ the original wholeness
→ and 對齊 take（原形）＋後面接名詞（受詞）→ 動詞原形 reconstruct
曾被 from 騙成「介系詞後接名詞」。

2. 先看詞性，再看意思

  1. 遮插入語
  2. 往前找 and／or／but：前後詞性要對齊（telling … and guiding、must take … and reconstruct）
  3. 往後看：後面接名詞 → 空格多半是動詞或形容詞；後面是 to＋動詞 → 想 For
  4. 把選項按詞性分組，每格只剩 1–3 個，再看語意

3. 同篇重複的字＝線索

  • Imperial Control：開頭 external domination → 結尾 foreign domination
  • SpaceX：第一句 reaching orbit → 後面 if it reached orbit
  • Chemistry：attract students → 同字根 attraction

4. 文法題 vs 語意題

  • 文法題：看空格旁的小字（逗號、into、is、to、by），答案有規則可推
  • 語意題：選項詞性都一樣、文法都通 → 看前後句意思、對比、因果

二、文法規則

1. , which 補充整件事

  • 逗號後面接補充說明 → 只能用 which，that 不能放逗號後
  • which 指前面整件事 → 動詞用單數：…, which makes it necessary
  • 口訣：, ____ ＋ 動詞 → which
  • 例：He passed the test, which made his mom happy.
情況用
逗號後補充只能 which
沒逗號、緊貼名詞that 或 which 都行
介系詞後面只能 which：the town in which I grew up

2. which 還是 what

  • 前面有名詞可以指 → which
  • 前面沒有名詞，空格本身＝「……的東西」→ what（＝the thing that）
  • What surprised me most was the price.　What I need most is more sleep.

3. that 當連接詞

agree／say／think／believe that ＋ 完整句子（這個 that 可省略）
Experts agree that the problem will get worse.　The manager said that there was no money left.

4. there 還是 it

看句子用例
只有名詞，意思是「有／沒有＿＿」ThereThere is no hospital nearby.
There is no doubt that…
後面有 to＋動詞 或 that＋完整句子（能搬到前面當主詞）ItIt is important to save money.
It is no wonder that…
It is no surprise that…
seems that ＋ 句子ItIt seems that the plan will not work.
seem(s) to be ＋ 名詞（有……）ThereThere seems to be less time for family.
指前面講過的東西ItThe noise? It seems to be a problem.
  • there 的動詞看後面名詞：There seems to be a problem（單數）／There seem to be fewer students（複數）
  • it 永遠單數：It seems，It seem ✗
  • no 後面接名詞：It is no surprise that… ✓／It is not surprising that… ✓／There is no surprising that… ✗（no＋形容詞沒講完）
  • within the industry 這種介系詞片語不能當主詞 → within the industry there is still uncertainty

5. 主動還是被動

  • 意思決定主被動：主詞自己做 → 主動；被別人做（常有 by）→ 被動
  • be＋p.p. 只是被動的長相，判定完才套
  • 同篇對照（Zika）：which is transmitted by mosquito bite（被蚊子傳 → 被動）／the virus circulated mostly in remote locations（自己流傳 → 主動）
  • rise 不接受詞，永遠不能被動
時態被動寫法
現在am／is／are＋p.p.
過去was／were＋p.p.
未來will be＋p.p.
現在完成has／have been＋p.p.
助動詞can／should／must be＋p.p.（It can be passed from mother to fetus）
做題四步：① 找主詞定單複數 ② 判主被動 ③ 看時間線索定時態 ④ 寫出動詞注意不規則

6. 時態線索＋過去式 vs 過去完成式

  • Today → 現在式；last week／last night／until last year／until recently → 過去式
  • 一件過去的事用過去式；兩件過去的事比先後，先發生的那件用 had＋p.p.
  • until 2020／until recently 只標結束時間，還是一件事 → 過去式
  • 看到 by the time／when／before ＋過去式 在比先後，才想 had＋p.p.：When I arrived, the train had left.
  • 並列對齊：Jasper Johns reimagined…; Rauschenberg employed…; Rivers used…（全過去式）

7. 不規則動詞

原形過去式p.p.注意
spreadspreadspread沒有 spreaded
riseroserisen沒有 rised；不能被動
sendsentsentThe letter was sent … by the school.
seeksoughtsoughtThey have sought to…

8. 名詞前要形容詞

  • the ____ material → hereditary（遺傳的），gene 是名詞塞不進去（「基因的」＝genetic）
  • a ____ biologist → molecular（分子的），molecule 是名詞
  • There is ____ difficulty → little
  • 字尾 -ary 常是形容詞：hereditary／necessary／ordinary
  • 名詞當形容詞用：success story、building block

9. 介系詞後面：名詞或 V-ing，不能原形

  • by bus／by the teacher／by 5 pm／by working hard ✓　by work hard ✗
  • Owls 篇：by converging on ideas
  • 其他介系詞同理：interested in learning、without saying goodbye、before leaving
  • to 的兩種身分：介系詞 → 接 V-ing（look forward to seeing、be used to living）；不定詞 → 接原形（want to go、for parties to be）

10. For someone to do something

  • ＝讓某人做某事。空格後面看到「誰＋to＋動詞」→ 填 For
  • although／because／if／when 後面要完整句子（主詞＋有時態的動詞）
  • 對照：For the plan to work, everyone must help.（要讓計畫成功）vs Although the plan worked, nobody thanked him.（雖然計畫成功了）
  • 兩種用法：句首 For you to pass, you must practise.／It is hard for me to wake up early.

11. 引述句型

  • 一段意見或預測（不一定有引號）＋逗號＋「說」類動詞＋說話者（人、報告、機構）
  • …, predicts Stephan Schuster＝Schuster predicts that…（倒裝）；代名詞放前面：…, he says
  • 「說」類：says／adds／warns／predicts／argues／notes／explains／suggests；不填「看」類 sees／foresees
  • predict＝pre 先＋dict 說；foresee＝fore 先＋see 看（腦中預見，不是說出來）
  • 不加 which："…," warns an expert 的主詞是 an expert；加 which 變「這句話警告了專家」

12. while 的兩個意思

意思判斷例
當……的時候兩件事同時發生I listened to music while I cooked.
而（對比）兩個不同主角在比較 → 空格跟前面相反Mountains restricted gene flow, while the Coast Range facilitated it.

13. 轉折與因果連接詞

  • Nevertheless＝然而（前後轉折）；Therefore＝因此（因果）
  • Thus＝因此（順著推結論）；After all＝畢竟（補理由，不是推結論）
  • Although 前後相反：雖然沒有斷層，但有幾個地形很重要（several）
  • survive and thus reproduce：thus 是副詞，放在 and 和動詞中間

14. 學術語氣：suggest 不是 prove

從一個觀察推結論 → 選語氣弱的 suggest／indicate／imply；prove＝證明，要決定性證據。
The river did not act as a barrier, suggesting owls readily fly over it.

15. 真動詞 vs V-ing／to V（讀長句）

  • 真動詞有時態：is／symbolizes／moved／will move
  • 單獨 V-ing 前面沒有 be → 不是動詞（動名詞或分詞）；to＋原形 也不是真動詞
  • It is an approach that symbolizes chemistry's success story: moving with the times, while holding fast to…
    → 真動詞只有 is 和 symbolizes；moving、holding 是補充
讀長句：
1. 先找主詞＋動詞，抓最短的完整句（It is an approach）
2. that／which → 修飾前面的名詞
3. 冒號 → 解釋前面的名詞
4. while／as／of → 補充細節，最後再看
容易看錯的字：
字以為其實
times次數時代（move with the times）
fast快的牢牢地（hold fast to）
advance動詞前進名詞進步（technological advance）
building蓋房子building block＝基石
popular受歡迎的人民的（popular movements）
address地址動詞：探討（The book addresses…）

16. 第三人稱 +s／複數 -es（頭號罩門）

  • 先找主詞，再決定動詞：The book addresses、it fits into、Stephan Schuster predicts、an approach that symbolizes、Understanding … teaches them
  • 字尾 -s／-ss／-sh／-ch／-x 加 -es：class→classes、teach→teaches、watch→watches、box→boxes、miss→misses

17. 要不要受詞

  • cut in＝插話，不接受詞：the listener can cut in at any time
  • cut off＝打斷某人，要接人：cut him off
  • fit into＝融入；adapt to＝適應（adapt 不接 into）

五、自測題（新例句）

先自己作答再點開。標 ★ 的是你練習時錯過的。

A. which／that／there／it／what

1. Prices rose by 20% last year, ____ forced many families to cut spending.
which　逗號後補充整件事
2. Experts agree ____ the problem will get worse.
that　連接詞＋完整句子
3. ____ is no doubt that online shopping has changed the way we live.
There　There is no doubt
★ 4. ____ is no wonder that young people prefer online shopping.
It　It is no wonder that（評論整件事）
★ 5. ____ seem to be fewer students in the library this year.
There　seem 沒 s → it 不行；fewer students 複數
6. The company, ____ was started in 1990, now has 5,000 workers.
which　公司是物，不用 who
7. ____ is important to save money for the future.
It　後面有 to save → It
8. The town in ____ I grew up has changed a lot.
which　介系詞後只能 which
★ 9. ____ surprised me most was the price.
What　前面沒名詞 → what＝the thing that
10. Within the city centre, ____ are very few places to park.
there　places 複數 → there are
11. He finally said sorry, ____ was exactly what everyone wanted.
which
12. ____ is no surprise that house prices keep going up.
It　no＋名詞 surprise
13. ____ seems that the plan will not work.
It　seems that＋句子 → It
14. In big cities, ____ seems to be less time for family.
there　seems to be＋名詞 → There；time 不可數 → seems
★ 15. In many small towns, ____ is still no hospital nearby.
there　只有名詞、意思是「沒有」→ There

B. 主被動＋時態

16. The news ____ (spread) quickly across the city last night.
spread　自己傳開＋過去
★ 17. The letter ____ (send) to every student by the school last week.
was sent　by → 被動；單數＋過去
★ 18. Today, the disease ____ (spread) by dirty water in many poor areas.
is spread　Today → 現在；spread 三態同形
★ 19. House prices ____ (rise) quickly until last year.
rose　rise→rose→risen；不能被動
20. Rumours ____ (circulate) only among local people until last month.
circulated　一件過去的事 → 過去式（had circulated 也通但非首選）
