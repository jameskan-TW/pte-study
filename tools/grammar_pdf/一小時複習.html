18 類（依頻率）

1s 的方向25 篇／44 次

先找主詞：單數動詞加 s，複數不加
  • s 像蹺蹺板：主詞有 s，動詞就沒有；people、children 也算複數
  • and 後面第二個動詞一樣看主詞；ch 結尾加 es（teaches）
long hours causes a bad temperlong hours cause…
a happy worker go home…worker goes home
he comes home early and keep……and keeps…
the effects of climate change is…effects… are

2介系詞片語25 篇／43 次

介系詞不照中文猜，整組背
  • pay attention to／in a better mood／focus on／interest in／addicted to／punished for／give up X for Y／put pressure on／have little effect on／surf the internet
pay more attention onpay more attention to
goes home with a good moodgoes home in a better mood
punished by each mistakepunished for each mistake

3可數名詞漏 s24 篇／57 次

可數名詞講一般情況：加 s
  • 先問「一個還是泛指？」一個 → a；泛指 → s
  • many／some 後接複數；很多人各一個 → their phones
old building are part of our historyold buildings are…
the translations of book…of books
a bad temper that hurts family…hurts families

4詞性用錯24 篇／39 次

be／feel／stay／keep 後接形容詞；our／leading to 後接名詞
  • worry→worried／health→healthy／anxiety→anxious／success→successful／relax→relaxed／body and mind／effect（名）vs affect（動）
  • 名詞後修飾：主動 -ing（people living in），被動 -ed
young people are worryare worried
pay attention to our healthyour health
keeps people safelykeeps people safe
people lived in the countrypeople living in…

5a／an 掉了23 篇／40 次

單數可數不裸奔，沒 a 就改複數
  • 句首最容易掉；兩個名詞各一個 a
  • 一個名詞兩個形容詞只要一個 a；no 佔冠詞位（no wage limit）
earn steady income／get promotiona steady income／a promotion
happy employee goes homeA happy employee…
a warm and a stable homea warm and stable home

6the 加錯／漏加21 篇／41 次

泛指不加 the，說得出「哪一個」才加
  • 加：the internet／the government／the law／the whole family／二選一 the better choice／特指 the information revolution
  • 不加：television（媒體）／society／peace of mind／take legal responsibility
the television does more harmTelevision does…
enjoy the peace of mindenjoy peace of mind
country life is a better choice…the better choice

7助動詞／to 後原形19 篇／23 次

should、can、will、to、crucial that 後面一律原形
  • ⑱ it is crucial that＋主詞＋原形：不加 s、不放 should
  • 動詞形態：⑧ 原形／⑭ +s／⑮ 原形／⑱ 原形
he can built a warm homecan build
crucial that students should finishcrucial that students finish
it will leads to stresswill lead

8不可數名詞17 篇／19 次

錢、資訊、工作、空氣、歷史：不加 a、不加 s，用 much／less
  • information／news／money／pay／work／knowledge／history／air／homework／advice
too many informationtoo much information
deal with the worksdeal with his work
fewer money／a cleaner airless money／cleaner air

9固定片語15 篇／20 次

收尾片語整串背，一字不換
  • a good job with a steady income／a warm and stable home／keeps the whole family in harmony／does more harm than good／a broader view of life
keeps family in harmonykeeps the whole family…
has more harm than gooddoes more harm than good
a stable incomea steady income

10動詞當主詞要 -ing14 篇／18 次

動詞當主詞先加 -ing，-ing 算單數，動詞加 s
  • 被動主詞：being＋過去分詞；一句一定要有真動詞
studying need timestudying needs time
been watch all the time leads…being watched…
students study old plays does…Studying old plays does…

11and／or13 篇／15 次

不能 A 也不能 B 用 or；三樣 A, B and C；and 兩邊對稱
cannot talk with you and listen…or listen
life is slow and far less pressure…slow and has far less pressure
clients and a steady income and warm homeclients, a steady income and a warm and stable home

12leading to 後接名詞11 篇／13 次

leading to 後只放名詞；and it 換成 that
  • before／for／without 後接名詞或 -ing
leading to have a broader viewleading to a broader view of life
a bad temper and it hurts familiesa bad temper that hurts families
before find a jobbefore finding a job

13比較級10 篇／12 次

可數 fewer、不可數 less；有 than 就要有 more
less working hours is less moneyfewer working hours mean less money
trust matters than discountsmatters more than
love matters on timelove matters more than timing

14逗號連兩句9 篇／11 次

兩個完整句不能只用逗號：, and／, but
  • 這裡不是 leading to，不要換成 that；that 子句要有主詞
…in order, a safe city keeps……in order, and a safe city…
…what they can do, cannot force them…, but cannot force them
things that cannot find in booksthings they cannot find

15make／let＋原形9 篇／10 次

make、let、help、see 直接原形；allow、push 要 to
makes employees to feel importantmakes employees feel…
help local family having…help local families earn…
allow their children use／push parents guidingallow… to use／push… to guide

16時態9 篇／10 次

一般情況現在式；James 做過的用過去式；if 子句現在式
people who lived alone can…people who live alone
As James gets marry after schoolAs James married…
if student surffing on internetIf a student surfs the internet

17every／there7 篇／10 次

every／each／everything 單數＋動詞 s；「有」用 there is／should be
protect every familiesprotect every family
everythings depend on moneyeverything depends on money
there have no wage limitthere should be no wage limit

18改一個掉一個5 篇／8 次

改完整句從頭念一次再送出
  • 範文只 5 篇，但 10/21–28 考題幾乎每批都有
allow their children use（上輪對、這輪掉 to）allow their children to use
with his families… with their parentwith his family… with his parents

送出前五步（每寫完一句跑一次）

  1. 找主詞決定動詞 s：單數加、複數不加；-ing 主詞、everything 算單數（1、10、17）
  2. 每個名詞過三關：可數嗎？一個（a）還是泛指（s）？說得出哪一個才加 the（3、5、6、8）
  3. be／feel／stay／keep 後是形容詞嗎？our／leading to 後是名詞嗎？（4、12）
  4. 介系詞和收尾片語是整組背的那個嗎？（2、9）
  5. 逗號兩邊是兩個完整句嗎？是就補 , and／, but（14）
